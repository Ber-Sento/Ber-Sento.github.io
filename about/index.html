About Me

Robotics development, engineering research, and practical problem solving.

I am a Mechanical Engineer with a strong interest in robotics, mechatronics, and automation. My work focuses on developing robotic systems by combining mechanical engineering principles, robot control, software development, and hands-on prototyping.

Throughout my undergraduate studies, I gained experience in robotics, mechatronics, and experimental engineering research. My academic work includes independently developing a slicing software system for a five-axis 3D printer, as well as contributing to experimental research on hydrogen fuel cells.

I also gained practical robotics experience during an internship involving a Kinova Gen3 robotic arm and an electric vehicle charging automation prototype. These experiences strengthened my interest in robotic manipulation, control, and the development of systems for practical applications.

I intend to pursue graduate research in Japan and continue developing my knowledge and capabilities in robotics and automation, with the long-term goal of contributing to robotics research and development.
