Projects

A collection of personal and engineering projects focused on robotics, embedded systems, automation, and practical prototyping. These projects reflect my interest in integrating mechanical design, electronics, and software into functional systems.

Desktop Companion Robot

Work in progress: an interactive desktop companion integrating embedded hardware and local AI.

2026

Developing a desktop companion system that combines an ESP32-S3 microcontroller, an OLED facial display, audio input and output, and locally hosted AI software. The project explores embedded system integration and natural interaction through voice and a simple visual interface. Hardware and software integration are currently in progress.

Interactive NFC integration within a functional 3D-printed structure.

May 2026

Designed and fabricated a custom 3D-printed NFC stand with embedded NTAG technology for interactive product presentation and structural testing of signal transmission through PLA.
