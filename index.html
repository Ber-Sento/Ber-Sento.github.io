Robotic EV Charging Automation

Robotics and Automation Internship | PT Cyber Teknologi Nusantara | July–September 2025

2025

Developed an EV charging automation prototype using a Kinova Gen3 6-DoF robotic arm, exploring the application of robotic manipulation to vehicle charging. During the internship, I experimented with Kinova's software for robot control and operation, and developed an application to operate the robotic arm. I also designed and 3D-printed supporting prototypes, including a charging station, a vehicle charging-port representation, and a charging gun for simulation.
