Robotic EV Charging Automation

Robotics and Automation Internship | PT Cyber Teknologi Nusantara

July–September 2025

Project Overview

This project explored the application of robotic manipulation to electric vehicle charging. The objective was to develop an EV charging automation prototype using a Kinova Gen3 6-DoF robotic arm, with the aim of allowing drivers to charge their vehicles without manually connecting the charging cable.

During my three-month internship at PT Cyber Teknologi Nusantara, I worked with the Kinova robotic arm and experimented with its software for robot control and operation. I also developed an application to operate the robotic arm and designed supporting physical prototypes for the charging station and vehicle-side components.

My Contributions

My work focused on robotic arm operation, application development, and the design and fabrication of supporting prototypes.

1. Robotic Arm Operation and Application Development

Worked with the Kinova Gen3 6-DoF robotic arm and explored its software for robot control and operation. I developed an application to record Cartesian coordinates and joint positions, while configuring motion parameters such as operating speed and force. This allowed the robot's movement positions and operating parameters to be set for the EV charging automation prototype.

2. Charging Station Prototype

Designed and fabricated a physical charging station prototype to support the development and demonstration of the robotic EV charging concept.

EV charging station prototype designed for the project.

3. Charging Gun and Vehicle Charging Port

Designed and 3D-printed a charging gun and a vehicle charging-port representation for use in the prototype. These components provided physical representations of the charging interface for the robotic manipulation concept.

3D-printed charging gun prototype.

Vehicle charging-port representation.

Experience and Technical Focus

This internship provided practical experience in applying robotic manipulation to an automation problem. It combined robotic arm operation and application development with mechanical design and physical prototyping.
